About Us

We opened Trailhead in 2019 with one grinder, one espresso machine, and a lot of optimism. Today we roast every batch ourselves and work directly with farmers in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala.

Every cup tells a story, and we think you should know it. Ask us about our sourcing any time.
